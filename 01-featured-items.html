Feature 01

Featured items module

The ask: content should feel more consistent — titles, summaries, imagery and spacing — and the team wants more flexibility over what appears.

Currently the “Latest thoughts” module on the home page. Today the items are different sizes, some carry images and some don’t, summaries run to different lengths, and a rotating statement sits in the middle of the grid.

Idea 1

Uniform card carousel

Closest to the Freshfields reference

Every item is the same card, in the same order, with the same fields. Consistency is enforced by the template rather than by editorial discipline. A carousel lets the module hold six to nine items without growing the page.

1Consistency comes from a fixed card contract.

  • Same four fields on every card: category tag, date, title, summary. No optional slots, so nothing can look half-finished.
  • Titles clamp to two lines, summaries to three, so a long title can never push a card out of alignment. Suggest a character guide in the CMS (title ≤ 75, summary ≤ 140).
  • No imagery at all in this option — which removes the biggest current inconsistency. Worth testing: the module reads as calmer, but loses colour on the page.
  • Flexibility: the carousel holds 6–9 items rather than 6 fixed tiles, and the “view all” gives a route to the full listing.
Idea 2

Lead item plus supporting feed, with curation controls

One deliberately chosen lead story carries the imagery; everything else is a clean text feed. This answers the flexibility ask directly — the module can run on “latest” automatically, or be hand-picked slot by slot.

Idea 3

Equal grid with content-type filters

No hierarchy at all — six identical tiles on a strict grid. Filters let the visitor narrow the module themselves, which reduces the pressure on editors to pick the perfect six.
