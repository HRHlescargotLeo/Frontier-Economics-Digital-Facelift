Featured items module

What changed: Idea 1’s uniform card carousel, now with an image in every card. The summary stays exactly as it was — it was doing the work the client valued, so it keeps its place and its three-line limit.

The image sits above the tag and title so the card reads top to bottom: picture, context, headline, summary, action. Every image uses the same 16:9 crop, which is what stops the module drifting back into the inconsistency it had before.

  • 1One crop, enforced by the template. Every image is 16:9 and rendered at the same width. Editors upload at any size; the CMS crops to a focal point they set once. This is the mechanism that keeps the module consistent — not editorial discipline.
  • 2A designed fallback for missing artwork. Where no image exists, the card shows a plain category block at the identical ratio rather than collapsing. The row stays level, so one item without a picture cannot break the module. In the live design this would be a Frontier brand colour keyed to the category.
  • Cards keep their fixed field set: image, category, date, title (2 lines), summary (3 lines), link. Nothing is optional, so no card can look half-finished.
  • The progress bar under the track shows how much of the set remains — worth having now that images make each card taller and fewer fit on screen.
