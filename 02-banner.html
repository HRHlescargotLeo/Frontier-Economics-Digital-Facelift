Below the fold
  • 1The proposition is text on a plain background. Nothing sits over the photography, so contrast is reliable and the message reads at any screen size. This is also what removes the overlap with the navigation you flagged — the image now starts below the tab row rather than running underneath it.
  • 2The image panel is fixed height, flexible width. It fills whatever space is left beside the copy, so a very wide monitor widens the picture rather than deepening the banner. Set one focal point per image and the crop follows it at every breakpoint.
  • 3Dots are clickable, not just indicators. Three featured stories rotate here; making them selectable means someone who spots a story part-way through can get back to it. Auto-rotation should pause on hover and stop entirely once a visitor interacts.
  • 4The featured items module exactly as signed off in Feature 01. Shown here in place so you can see the two working together — same cards, same 16:9 crop, same carousel controls.
  • Above the fold on a standard laptop: the proposition, both calls to action, one featured story and the first row of featured items down to the summary line. Roughly 480px recovered against the current banner.
  • On mobile the two halves stack — copy first, then the image with the story card beneath it rather than overlaid, since an overlay at that width leaves too little room for either.
