People directory — landing page

What changed: the layout, filter rail and descriptive cards from Idea 1, with the prominent expertise-aware search from Idea 3 lifted to the top of the page.

The two mechanisms now sit on separate jobs rather than competing. Search answers “I know roughly who or what I want.” The rail and grid answer “show me who could help.” Neither hides the other.

  • 1Suggestions appear as you type — try “ener”, “merg” or a surname. The field is empty on load and the list opens over the content beneath rather than pushing it down, so the search sits tight against the results and nothing moves on the page. Suggestions start at two characters; below that the list stays closed.
  • Expertise results apply as a filter. Pick “Energy” or “Regulation” from the list and watch the rail, the chips and the count update — the visitor stays on the page rather than being thrown to a separate results screen. Search and filters are one system, not two.
  • 2Popular searches seed the box. Click one to see it typed in and matched. They double as a statement of what Frontier works on, and over time they tell us what visitors actually look for, which should drive the filter order.
  • The no-match state is written, not blank. Type something like “xyz” to see it. Arrow-down moves into the list and Escape closes it, so the field works without a mouse.
  • 3Count and chips are always visible. The current overlay panel hides filter state as soon as it closes; keeping it in view is the single biggest usability gain here.
  • 4The one-line summary, in the person’s own voice. This is the field that makes the directory browsable, and it is reused verbatim on the profile page (Feature 04).
  • 5The card works without it. With 423 people the summary will not be written everywhere at launch, so a card with no summary falls back to sector and service tags rather than leaving a gap. Suggest a limit of 110 characters so no card runs to more than two lines.
  • “Show more” replaces 34 pages of pagination. Filters and search terms should be held in the URL so a filtered view can be sent to a colleague or a client.
