Feature 04

People profile

The ask: remove the Q&A and replace it with something that guides people to content related to that profile — blogs, articles and similar — where such content exists. The aggregator should be built so that a reduced version can be reused elsewhere on the site.

Based on Moritz Absenger’s profile. Content shown in the aggregators below is indicative, drawn from Frontier’s energy and regulation publications.

Idea 1

Sticky contact rail with a stacked profile

Contact details and expertise stay on screen the whole way down, so “get in touch” is never more than a glance away. The Q&A slot is taken by the content aggregator, which sits directly after the biography.

How one component serves all three.

  • Source — articles, news, case studies, events, or any combination.
  • Filter — by person, by sector, by service, or hand-picked. This is what lets the same block sit on a profile, a sector page or an article footer without a new build each time.
  • Presentation — full, reduced or compact, chosen per placement.
  • Fallback — if the filter returns nothing, widen to the parent sector, or hide the block. Set once per placement so no page can render an empty heading.
